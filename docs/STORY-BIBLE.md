# Under the Glow — Story Bible v1
**No-art doctrine:** Fear from rules, text, sound, pressure — not pictures.

## Premise
The house learned how to notice. The TV’s static is the wound that never closes. You return under the glow whenever the house wins.

## Player
Someone who hid. The house kept the habit of looking for them.

## Core states
- **Seen** — reflection / attention (mirror)
- **Marked** — the house files you (fog letter / label)
- **Named** — you gave it a handle (talk at mirror)

## Entity
Optional. Arrives when the *tune* is wrong. Not explained. Architecture remains the primary threat.

## Laws
1. Death always returns to the TV glow.
2. Looking back can stretch space.
3. Mercy is scarce (one true hide per run).
4. Map and memory may soften lies.

## Endings
- **A — Carpet:** seen + marked + named → static thins; living room flicker; carpet.
- **B — Still glowing:** named but always fled/hid from danger → you wake under the glow on purpose.
